# TaskNest Billing Policy

## Plans and pricing
- Free: $0, up to 3 projects.
- Pro: $12 per user per month, or $120 per user per year.
- Business: $25 per user per month, or $250 per user per year, minimum 5 seats.
Prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes.

## Billing cycle
Subscriptions renew automatically on the same date each billing period. An invoice is emailed to the account owner on the renewal date and is available under Settings > Billing > Invoices.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Bank transfer is available only for Business annual plans. Payment card details can only be changed by the account owner in Settings > Billing. Support agents cannot view full card numbers or change card details on your behalf.

## Failed payments
If a renewal payment fails, we retry 3 times: on day 1, day 3, and day 7. The subscription is marked "past due" during this period. If the payment is still unpaid on day 8, the workspace becomes read-only.

## Duplicate or unexpected charges
If you were charged twice on the same day, one of the two entries is often a temporary authorization hold placed by your bank, not a second charge. Holds usually disappear within 3-5 business days. If both charges show as completed (settled) after 5 business days, it is a genuine duplicate charge and support will investigate. A duplicate charge confirmed to be our error is refunded in full to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.

## Changing or cancelling a plan
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.
