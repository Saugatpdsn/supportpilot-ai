# TaskNest Account Access Guide

## Resetting your password
On the login page choose "Forgot password" and enter your account email. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used only once. Check your spam folder. If nothing arrives within 10 minutes, request a new link. You can request at most 5 reset links per hour.

## Account lockout
After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. You can wait, or reset your password to unlock it sooner.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
Enable 2FA in Settings > Security. Recovery codes are shown once at setup, so store them safely. If you lost both your device and your recovery codes, a human support agent must verify your identity manually. Automated assistants cannot disable or bypass 2FA.

## Changing your email address
Go to Settings > Profile > Email. A confirmation link is sent to both the old and the new address.

## Deleting your account
Only the account owner can delete an account, and every deletion request requires human review. Data is kept for a 30-day grace period, after which deletion is permanent.

## Security reminder
TaskNest support will never ask for your password, full card number, or 2FA codes.
