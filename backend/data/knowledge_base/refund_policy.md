# TaskNest Refund Policy

## Eligibility
- Monthly plans: no refunds for partial months. You can cancel at any time to stop future charges.
- Annual plans: a full refund is available if requested within 14 days of the initial purchase or renewal date. After 14 days, annual plans are not refundable, except for duplicate charges or billing errors caused by TaskNest.
- Cancelling a plan does not automatically issue a refund.

## How to request a refund
Every refund request must be reviewed and approved by a human support agent. The customer should provide the order ID and the reason for the request. Agents review refund requests within 2 business days.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-10 business days. TaskNest cannot refund to a different card, and cannot pay refunds by cash or bank transfer.

## Not refundable
- Add-on purchases such as extra storage.
- Accounts suspended for violating the Terms of Service.
