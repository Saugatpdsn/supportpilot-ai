# TaskNest Product FAQ

## What is TaskNest?
TaskNest is a project and task management tool for teams, with boards, timelines, and integrations.

## Plan comparison
- Free: 3 projects, 5 GB storage, community support only.
- Pro: unlimited projects, 100 GB storage, integrations, API access, email support with a 24-hour response time.
- Business: everything in Pro plus 1 TB storage, SAML single sign-on (SSO), audit logs, admin roles, and priority support with a 4-hour response time.

## Free trial
Pro and Business include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start.

## Exporting data
All plans can export data as CSV or JSON under Settings > Data.

## Mobile apps
TaskNest has iOS and Android apps. Offline mode is not supported.

## Data location and security
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You choose a US or EU data region when creating a workspace, and it cannot be changed afterward.

## Discounts
Nonprofits and students receive 50% off Pro annual plans through the discount application form. Verification is required.
